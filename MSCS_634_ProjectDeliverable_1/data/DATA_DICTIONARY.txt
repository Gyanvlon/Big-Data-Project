Berkeley Earth city panel data dictionary

Original seven attributes
  dt: observation month, ISO date (first day of month).
  AverageTemperature: monthly mean temperature in degrees Celsius.
  AverageTemperatureUncertainty: source-reported uncertainty in Celsius;
    retain the field without treating it as independent observations or
    interpreting it as a forecast interval.
  City: source-native city name (e.g., Peking and Bombay).
  Country: source-native country name.
  Latitude: source coordinate with N/S hemisphere suffix.
  Longitude: source coordinate with E/W hemisphere suffix.

Four joined source attributes from GlobalTemperatures.csv
  LandAverageTemperature: global monthly mean land temperature, Celsius.
  LandMaxTemperature: global monthly average maximum land temperature, Celsius.
  LandMinTemperature: global monthly average minimum land temperature, Celsius.
  LandAndOceanAverageTemperature: global monthly mean land/ocean temperature, Celsius.
  Join: many city-month rows to one global month on dt, without adding rows.
  These are sourced global context, repeated across cities, not new local
  measurements. All four current-month values are excluded from forecast X.

Derived analytical attributes
  date: parsed datetime version of dt.
  city_id: City and Country combined to avoid ambiguous city names.
  latitude / longitude: numeric signed degrees, south/west negative.
  year / month: calendar year and month (1-12).
  climatology: city-calendar-month mean for 1951-1980, in Celsius.
  baseline_count: available baseline observations per city-calendar-month.
  anomaly: AverageTemperature minus climatology, in Celsius (regression target).
  lag1_anomaly: same city's previous calendar-month anomaly, Celsius.
  lag12_anomaly: same city's anomaly 12 calendar months earlier, Celsius.
  prior12_mean: mean of preceding 12 anomalies, with at least 9 available.
  lag1_uncertainty: previous calendar-month reported uncertainty, Celsius.
  month_sin / month_cos: cyclic encoding of calendar month.
  time_years: years since January 1981, including fractional month.
  abs_latitude: absolute latitude in degrees.
  hemisphere: North or South from numeric latitude.
  warm_month: 1 if anomaly strictly above +1 C; otherwise 0 (classification
    target, not a heatwave definition or an international warming target).

Only the eleven documented model features enter the supervised pipelines.
Current temperature, anomaly, uncertainty, climatology and warm_month are
excluded from predictors. Derived columns are generated reproducibly in each
notebook and are not added independent measurements. Source subset and
provenance are bundled for offline reproduction.
